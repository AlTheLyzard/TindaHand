# Authentication backgrounds

`background.png` and `header-background.png` are unchanged copies of the supplied `docs/designs/ApplicationBackground.png` and `ApplicationHeaderBackground.png`. They contain the waves/store illustration only; logo, text, cards, fields, buttons, success indicators and navigation remain native UI. Neither asset contains a fake status bar or home indicator.

## Recovery illustration

`recovery-envelope.svg` traces the open-envelope outline in the fourth panel of
`docs/designs/MA-LOG-006.png` in its original #28549A. No standalone envelope asset
was supplied. The SVG keeps the reference's rounded outline, open flap and square
proportions with a transparent background; the toast and device chrome are omitted.
