# **USER REQUIREMENTS:**

1. **May-Ari / Store Owners** \- these are the users who will take on the Admin role. They are responsible for managing the overall operations, transactions, inventory, and financial activities of the sari-sari store through TindaHand. They can register and manage their own account, set up Kahera accounts, monitor store sales and cash, manage products, stocks, and assets, process sales, manage customer and store debts, and record store expenses. They can also access financial statements and fully manage transaction records to monitor and organize the store’s financial activities.  
   

	**Allowed to manage / actions:**

* Register and access the May-Ari account  
* Set up and manage Kahera accounts and access permissions  
* Monitor Pera sa Kaha / Money Tracker  
* View Sales Reports and daily, weekly, and monthly summaries  
* Manage Paninda / Inventory, including products and quantities, prices, categories, stock levels, restocking, and expiration dates  
* Manage Pag-aari / Assets, including asset classification, value, and records  
* Process sales through the POS, including Cash and GCash payments  
* Manage Sisingilin / Customer Debts, including debt records, payments, due dates, and payment history  
* Manage Utang Ko / Store Debts, including creditors, payments, due dates, and payment history  
* Manage Gastusin / Store Expenses, including recording, categorizing, monitoring, and viewing expense history  
* Use Quick Add for New Sale, Add Paninda, Add Pag-aari, Record Sisingilin, Record Utang Ko, and Add Gastusin  
* Generate and download Financial Statements, including General Journal, General Ledger, Income Statement, and Balance Sheet  
* Fully manage Transaction History, including viewing, searching, filtering, editing, updating, and deleting transactions  
* Use the Barcode Scanner to identify specific and selected products and modify the product quantities  
* Manage Profile and Settings, including account information, application preferences, and notification preferences

2. **Kahera / Store Operator** \- these are the users who will take on the User role. They are the ones who assist the May-Ari in handling day-to-day operations of the sari-sari store. They access the application using their assigned Kahera PIN and can perform operational tasks such as processing sales, managing products and stocks, handling customer debts, and monitoring sales transactions. Their access focuses on daily store operations, while financial statements, expenses, user access, and certain management features are reserved for the May-Ari.  
   

	**Allowed to manage / actions:**

* Select the Kahera profile and log in using the assigned May-Ari ID and PIN  
* Process sales through the POS, including Cash and GCash payments  
* View and manage Inventory Management / Paninda, including product quantities, prices, categories, restocking, product searching and monitoring, and expiration dates  
* Manage Sisingilin / Customer Debts, including debt records, payments, due dates, and payment history  
* Use Quick Add for New Sale and Record Sisingilin  
* View, search, and filter Transaction History  
* Use the Barcode Scanner to identify specific and selected products and modify the product quantities.  
* Manage their Profile and Settings  
* Cannot access Financial Statements, Gastusin / Store Expenses, and Access Control; authorized only for May-Ari

# **SYSTEM FEATURES:**

**May-Ari Module**

1. Login / Account Access \- this allows May-Ari to create an account (if a new user), set up a Kahera account, and log in to their account with their May-Ari ID and PIN.  
2. Dashboard \- this allows May-Ari to view Pera sa Kaha / Money Tracker, view Sales Report summarized daily, weekly, and monthly, view Stock Health by Out of Stock and Low Stock, view Mga Mabentang Items / Best-Selling Items, view Recent Activity, click on Quick Add to add New Sale, Add Paninda, Add Pag-aari, Record Sisingilin, Record Utang Ko, and Add Gastusin. Use the bottom navigation bar, which includes the Home, POS, Inventory and Asset Management, and Menu buttons.  
3. Point-of-Sale (POS) \- this allows May-Ari to process sales by adding a product to the cart, viewing the product’s price and quantity, searching for a specific product, choosing the specific product category, reviewing the products in the cart, adding and/or removing a product in the cart, viewing the total amount to pay, choosing a payment method either Cash or GCash, attaching the GCash Reference Number, if applicable, and confirming payment for a successful sale.  
4. Inventory and Asset Management \- this allows the May-Ari to search for a specific product, use the Barcode Scanner to retrieve product information and modify product quantities, view the Inventory Dashboard, which includes Total products and Total inventory value, view product stock category: good, low stock, out-of-stock, and nearing expiry, add new product, add restocked product, edit product information, view Asset Dashboard, add asset, classify asset: Equipment or Furniture and Fixtures, edit asset information.  
5. Accounts Receivable and Accounts Payable / Customer Debt / Sisingilin and Store Debt / Utang Ko \- this allows the May-Ari to view Sisingilin Overview, add and record customer debt, record debt amount, record payment deadline, record payment, view payment history, confirm payment made, view Store Debt Overview, add and record store debt, record store debt amount, record payment, confirm payment made, and view payment history.  
6. Quick Add \- this allows the May-Ari to manage common store operations more efficiently and more accessible, add New Sale, add Paninda and Pag-aari, add Sisingilin and Utang Ko, without having to go manually and look for the specific component they need to access. (Barcode Scanner TBA)  
7. More Tools \- this allows the May-Ari to access the Financial Statement Generation page to generate General Journal, General Ledger, Income Statement, and Balance Sheet, download them in PDF or JPG/JPEG format, access the Transaction History page, view and make necessary changes when needed to specific transaction records, access Gastusin / Store Expense, and add and record store expenses.  
8. Profile \- this allows the May-Ari to view and manage profile information for both May-Ari and Kahera profiles, manage Kahera authorized access and permissions, and log out of the account.  
9. Settings \- this allows the May-Ari to manage application settings like app and account customization and notification preferences.

**Kahera Module**

1. Login / Account Access \- this allows Kahera to log in to their account using the provided May-Ari ID and Kahera PIN  
2. Point-of-Sale (POS) \- this allows Kahera to process sales by adding a product to the cart, viewing the product’s price and quantity, searching for a specific product, choosing the specific product category, reviewing the products in the cart, adding and/or removing a product in the cart, viewing the total amount to pay, choosing a payment method either Cash or GCash, attaching the GCash Reference Number, if applicable, and confirming payment for a successful sale  
3. Inventory Management \- this allows the Kahera to search for a specific product, use the Barcode Scanner to retrieve product information and modify product quantities, view the Inventory Dashboard, which includes Total products and Total inventory value, view product stock category: good, low stock, out-of-stock, and nearing expiry, add new product, add restocked product, and edit product information  
4. Accounts Receivable / Customer Debt / Sisingilin \- this allows the Kahera to view Sisingilin Overview, add and record customer debt, record debt amount, record payment deadline, record payment, view payment history, and confirm payment made  
5. Quick Add \- this allows the Kahera to manage common store operations more efficiently and more accessibly, add New Sale, and add Paninda. (Barcode Scanner TBA)  
6. More Tools \- this allows the Kahera to access the Transaction History page, search and filter through transaction records  
7. Profile \- this allows the Kahera to view and manage profile information for their own profiles, edit available information when available, and log out of the account.  
8. Settings \- this allows the Kahera to manage application settings like app and account customization and notification preferences.
