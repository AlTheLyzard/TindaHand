## Confirmed rules and source differences

- The updated task supersedes inconsistent screenshot amounts, customers, percentages, dates and selected navigation. Cards prominently show outstanding amounts; progress is confirmed paid/original. An overdue partial debt stays Overdue. Due today is current; an absent POS due date never becomes overdue. Relative labels use device-local calendar dates and UTC arithmetic on date components, avoiding daylight-saving-hour differences.
- Existing Quick Add/customer API notes require a valid due date and default it to 30 calendar days. Most AR frames agree; one product frame says Optional. The full form retains the documented required-date rule, including for products. No new minimum/maximum due-date policy is imposed. Existing POS debt deadlines remain null.
- Product-debt stock deduction is explicitly authorized by this task, superseding older record-only Quick Add and unresolved stock-effect notes. Stock changes once, only with successful debt creation. No sales, cash-register movement, expense or other accounting entry is inferred.
- Enter/select follows the existing name-only customer shape and shared registry. To meet the confirmed prohibition on silent name merges, AR and Quick Add require selecting an existing ID to link that person; typed names visibly create a new identity on save, including same-name people. POS keeps its established normalized-name linking rules and explicit selection controls. Same-name customer choices show their IDs.
- Baseline MA/KH-AR-004 asks for GCash references when applicable. The new AR payment frames and existing contracts have no method/reference input. AR records only amount and timestamp; the initial GCash reference from an existing POS partial sale is preserved and displayed in history. Hidden Cash-mode reference drafts are excluded. A method/reference contract for future AR collections remains open.
- The copy owner sheet adds a debt status label and unresolved item-checkbox repayment, receipt-page and future permission remarks. These are not merged into the baseline. The confirmed selected-debt amount-based payment rule governs this flow.
- Both role sheets describe receivable access. The checkout currently implements only an owner session, with no functional cashier authentication/store identity. Existing route/session gates remain enforced, including direct URLs. This task does not add cashier authentication or grant owner financial capabilities to cashiers; shared AR components and types can serve that future session adapter.

## State and integration boundary

`src/types/receivables.ts` defines confirmed payments and creation/payment commands. `src/data/receivables.ts` supplies fictional, independently identified borrowings and payment entries; fixtures are cloned into session state. `src/utils/receivables.ts` derives balances, exclusive statuses, search results, totals and counts, and implements immutable commits. `src/services/receivables.ts` is the replaceable mock repository boundary; it delegates to the existing inventory service/action pipeline.

The inventory session's `pos.debts` is now the single customer-debt ledger used by full-page AR, Customer Debt Quick Add and POS partial checkout. Its `receivablePayments` stores separate confirmed events. Quick Add's provider routes customer saves to the receivable service and exposes the same records to existing summaries. Expense and payable stores remain separate. Historical POS sale/tender snapshots retain their checkout-time values; a later collection updates the linked debt, without re-deducting stock or rewriting the original sale.

Creation rechecks current stock, valid quantities, selected customer IDs, exact positive centavos and dates before atomically appending the debt/customer and deducting products. Items retain their recorded names and selling prices. Payment confirmation rechecks the selected owner's debt balance, appends one event and updates the compatible remaining-balance record fields in one state change. Replayed save/payment tokens return their original result without another mutation. Frontend guards are simulation, not production authorization or durable idempotency.

For backend integration, replace the service with authenticated store/role-scoped reads and atomic debt/customer/stock and payment transactions, durable idempotency/reconciliation, server-owned totals, validated price snapshots, canonical customer identities and persistence. No HTTP endpoint or backend technology was invented. Agree payment method/reference and accounting posting contracts before adding those fields or cash effects. AR/POS state follows the existing inventory session lifetime and resets on logout/reload; AP/expense cache behavior is unchanged.
