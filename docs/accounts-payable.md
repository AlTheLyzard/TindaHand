# Accounts Payable — MA-AP-001–004

Frontend-only implementation based on the supplied `docs/designs/AP-*.png` frames and Accounts Receivable overview patterns. The named MAY-ARI MODULE sheet in the original requirements workbook remains the baseline; source requirements are unchanged.

Account Balances lives at `/owner-menu/receivables-payables`. Payable has Unpaid/Paid tabs, creditor search, Overdue/Pending/Remaining Balance groups, a full pending list, business-debt creation, creditor selection, debt details and payment entry. Unpaid and paid cards open dedicated creditor details. All nested routes inherit the owner gate; cashier login does not grant an owner identity.

The unpaid summary includes every open debt regardless of search and shows both open transaction count and distinct creditor count. Overdue means an unpaid balance with a due date before the current Manila date; overdue takes precedence over pending/partial payment state. Pending means no confirmed payments and not overdue. Remaining Balance means a partial payment with a positive balance and no overdue date. Cards show the remaining balance, due date/relative status, payment progress and paid-vs-original amount. The overview shows up to the first two Pending cards with a View all action whenever that group is shown, plus pull-to-refresh for the Manila date used in relative status labels. Search filters both Unpaid and Paid names. The paid summary appears above the status tabs and includes only fully settled debts matching the search, at their original settled value. Saving a debt returns through confirmation and success dialogs; the overview does not show a banner or toast above its unpaid summary.

New Business Debt accepts a typed or selected creditor, positive exact-centavo amount, optional due date and notes. The creditor picker opens from the single person-outline trailing icon; the optional date uses a calendar field without a Clear Due Date action. Field errors appear after invalid values lose focus and Save follows the AR enabled/disabled behavior. A confirmation dialog precedes saving and a success dialog appears before returning to the overview. The form remains mounted beneath Creditor Names so selection preserves other fields. Save tokens and synchronous tap guards prevent duplicate saves. Cancel/back creates no record. The full form starts with no due date.

`createQuickAddService` delegates Store Debt to `createPayablesRepository`. `useQuickAdd` exposes the same ledger to both entry points; its combined records adapt payables to the existing remaining-balance convention. Other Quick Add variants and POS customer debts keep their existing behavior. Payable creditors, debts and payment events have stable IDs, integer-centavo amounts, date-only due dates and ISO event timestamps. Seeds are cloned into editable repository state, filtered by owner, and are never mutated. Selected IDs are validated; typed names reuse normalized existing identities as a demo convention (same-name real suppliers need a backend identity policy).

Payment typing and fixed ₱500/₱1,000/₱2,000 presets only update a local preview. Full Payment fills the exact outstanding amount; presets above the balance are disabled. A confirmation dialog revalidates against current repository history, appends one event and then shows a success dialog. Full settlement returns to Paid; partial payment returns to Unpaid. Creditor Debt Details aggregates the creditor's matching debt records, shows the calculated remaining balance, date-grouped borrowing information and chronological payment history. Paid Details shows a ₱0.00 remaining balance, total amount and payment history, and omits Record Payment after settlement.

The supplied AP entry form has no borrowing-type selector. The mock ledger therefore displays new form entries as Cash by default and only shows Goods/Stock for fixture entries marked that way. `borrowingType` is client-only; it does not define or change a backend schema.

The repository is a replaceable in-memory boundary. No backend requests, persistence, inventory changes, expenses, cash movements, interest or allocation rules are introduced. Records survive navigation and retain the existing provider's owner-filtered session lifetime; app reload resets them. Future integration needs server authorization, atomic writes, durable idempotency and canonical records before success feedback.

Requirements gaps: MA-AP-004 mentions a GCash reference when applicable, but the updated AP form has no payment method/reference fields and no AP schema defines them. Payments therefore record amount and timestamp only; no method is inferred. The copy owner sheet adds overview search/payment-history remarks, replaces validation with a cancellation remark, and still marks MA-AP-003/004 “To be designed.” These differences are not merged into the baseline; updated supplied designs and the explicit task decisions govern this implementation.
